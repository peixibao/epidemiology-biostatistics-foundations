## What was formally taught in 1.1B
- Four common research aims: **descriptive, associational, causal, predictive**; neither exhaustive nor mutually exclusive.
- Definitions, aims and examples under one common topic (physical activity and cardiovascular health).
- Descriptive: frequency/distribution in defined population; association: observed relationships and confounding; causal: intervention contrast and potential outcomes; prediction: performance in new data, predictors vs causes.
- Distinction between **research aims**, **study designs**, and **statistical methods**; same regression method may be used for multiple purposes.
- Independent generic example: carrying a lighter, lung cancer, and smoking — association/prediction without causal effect of giving a lighter.
- Causal studies may be experimental or observational under differing assumptions.
